Evidence basis: Live crawls (robots.txt + sitemap), Ahrefs via authenticated Chrome (domain overview + top pages), live HTML inspection. GSC for source sites = BLOCKED (see Assumptions & Blockers).

Key risks:

  1. Blume's 42K backlinks. 46% of crawled pages return 3XX and a large share of links point to /blog and /gallery; these must be 301-mapped or consolidated, never dropped to the homepage.
  2. Gallery consolidation. 500+ before/after gallery URLs (Blume 310, Ageless 55) carry visual-equity content but almost no search value; retire individually, preserve media into a single per-location gallery.
  3. Influx export path is UNVERIFIED. No backend access yet; playbook assumes worst-case crawl-assisted migration until a structured export is confirmed (see Section 3).
  4. Manor destination is mid-build. Only 25 services + 5 locations + 61 blog URLs live; the new service/location pages must be BUILT before their redirects can launch.
  5. GSC unavailable for all 6 source sites. Post-launch validation for those domains cannot rely on first-party GSC; Ahrefs + live crawl are primary.

Recommended sequencing: Blume first (highest value, validate the playbook), then Aere/Marvel (multi-location), then Ageless/All About Me, then retire the Manor legacy site. ~7 days observation between waves (inherited Manor methodology).

The decision: two approaches plus a hybrid

The six sites can be migrated into Manor using two different service architectures. Both preserve the same traffic and the same location pages. They differ only in how service pages are organized. A third, hybrid option combines them.

Option A: Location-scoped services (matches prior Manor playbooks)

/locations/{city}/services/{service}/
/locations/{city}/providers/{provider}/

A dedicated service page for every city, with city-qualified slugs (for example /locations/scottsdale/services/botox-scottsdale-az/). Strongest for local search and GBP alignment, but ~150 pages to build and maintain. Effort ~315-410 hours.

Option B: Global consolidated services (matches Manor's live build)

/services/{service}/
/providers/{provider}/

One strong national page per service, absorbing all six sites. Fewer pages (~65), cheaper (~256-329 hours), and matches what Manor serves today, but weaker on city-specific service queries and diverges from the locked playbook.

Hybrid (recommended)

  • Location-scoped services for the markets that actually rank locally: Scottsdale (Blume), Denver and Beverly Hills (Aere), and Marvel's Tennessee cities.
  • Global consolidated services for thin or overlapping services and secondary locations.
  • ~90-100 pages, ~350-364 internal hours.

This concentrates page-building where the equity is and avoids multiplying thin pages everywhere else.

Migration path (preference order)

  1. Vendor-supported structured export (ACCESS REQUIRED, request from Influx/Candid Pulse). Covers content, media, SEO fields, providers, redirects.
  2. Crawl-assisted extraction (fallback: screaming-frog-cli JS-render crawl + crawl4ai body recovery). HTML + title/desc/H1 + media URLs + internal links recovered.
  3. Manual rebuild only for content that is unique and high-value and unrecoverable by the above (expected under 5% of pages).
