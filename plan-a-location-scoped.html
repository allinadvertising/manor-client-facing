PLAN A - Location-Scoped Architecture (Follows Manor Precedent)

Destination: https://thisismanor.com/ Scope: 6 Influx CMS sites to WordPress. 1,038 source URLs, ~10 physical locations, ~50 unique service types.

Architecture (locked)

/locations/{city}/                        <- one page per physical location
/locations/{city}/services/{service}/     <- location-qualified service pages
/locations/{city}/providers/{provider}/   <- location-qualified provider pages
/services/{service}/                      <- national service hub (thin, links down to locations)
/blog/{post}/                             <- consolidated editorial
/gallery/{location}/                      <- one gallery per location

City-qualified slugs follow the precedent, e.g. /locations/scottsdale/services/botox-scottsdale-az/, /locations/santa-fe/services/rf-microneedling-santa-fe-nm/.

Why this plan

Matches the stated national-brand-with-local-practice strategy in the Modern SLC and Vitalyc game plans. Local service demand ("botox scottsdale", "med spa santa fe") is captured on a dedicated local page rather than a national page. GBP points at the local location page.

Adapted time estimates (hours)

Scope drivers vs precedent: Modern SLC = 1 location / 142 URLs / 18 pages (113.5 h). Vitalyc = 5 locations / 153 URLs / 48-60 pages (180-210 h). This engagement = ~10 locations / 1,038 URLs / ~150 pages, plus Influx CMS extraction (new, not present in either precedent).

Sequencing

Same waves/gates as the shared methodology, but redirects are simpler 1:1 city-to-city (each source service to its city-qualified Manor equivalent). Blume first (control site), then Aere/Marvel (multi-location), then Ageless/AllAboutMe, then retire legacy Manor.

Key risks

  1. Thin location-service pages. 120 pages risk low depth if the services-offered matrix over-reaches. Mitigate by building only Priority-1 services with real local demand and a confirmed offered service (Vitalyc precedent: 24-60 range, not all services everywhere).
  2. Higher build + copy cost (~150 pages vs ~65).
  3. Ongoing maintenance. Every new acquisition multiplies pages; the template must be strict.
