03 · The decision

Two ways to structure the new Manor pages

Both preserve the same traffic. They differ in how service pages are organized and how much needs to be built.

Location-scoped services

A dedicated service page for every city, matching the prior Manor migration playbooks and strongest for local search.

05 · Recommendation

Pros, cons, and a suggested path

Option A · Location-scoped
  • Matches the prior Manor playbooks
  • Strongest for local queries like "botox scottsdale"
  • Clean Google Business Profile alignment
  • Simpler one-to-one redirects
  • ~85 more pages to build and maintain
  • Risk of thin pages if over-built
  • Requires rebuilding a layer Manor no longer serves
Option B · Global services
  • Matches what Manor serves today
  • Fewer, stronger consolidated pages
  • ~84 hours cheaper and faster to launch
  • Scales cleanly with future acquisitions
  • Weaker on city-specific service queries
  • Diverges from the locked playbook
  • Merge work can drop content if not disciplined
Suggested

A hybrid, focused where the equity is

Build location-scoped services only for the markets that actually rank locally (Scottsdale, Denver, Beverly Hills, and Marvel's Tennessee cities), and consolidate everything else into strong national pages.

~90–100 pages · ~350–364 internal hours
